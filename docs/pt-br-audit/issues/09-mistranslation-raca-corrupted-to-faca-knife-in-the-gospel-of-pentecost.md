# [pt-BR][mistranslation] `raca` corrupted to `faca` ("knife") in the Gospel of Pentecost V

**Labels:** bug, good first issue

`missa/Portugues/Tempora/Pent05-0.txt:36`, `[Evangelium]` (Mt 5:20-24).

Latin:
```
Qui autem díxerit fratri suo, raca: reus erit concílio.
```

Portuguese:
```
...aquele que disser a seu irmão faca será réu no conselho (Sinédrio)...
```

*Raca* is an Aramaic term of contempt left untranslated in the Vulgate. It has been corrupted into `faca` ("knife"), producing "whoever says to his brother 'knife'".
